# Piggyback over a whole shift

Each policy works a 24-hour shift hour by hour: it sees only what was recorded by each hour, books recoveries that hold truck space for the rest of the shift, and gets parcels back when a booking breaks (noticed 2 h later, up to 3 bookings per parcel). 20 paired shifts (seeds x capacity tightness). Generated by `python -m benchmark.shift`.
